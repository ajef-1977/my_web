About US

QUENTAIN CORPORATION

was founded in 1997, as a professional PP stationary manufacturer and distributor. Since its first production of PP dining mats in 2002 as a demand from one of our customers, our mats have received outstanding reviews and became popular in the international market.


Since then, our main production focus has shifted to PP mat various vinyl mat & runner and PE(friendly material) mats due to constant orders mats by our customers. Achieving high standard quality to meet our customers’satisfaction has always been our mission.

All customers are welcome to provide their own designs.

All designs are highly secured and will not be shared with any third –party.

we believe that you will find our products and service meeting your needs.
